# Basista map display, boundaries and reporting scope

9 November 2026, Philippine time. The user confirmed management by **Basista Municipal Hall** and coverage of exactly **13 barangays**: Anambongan, Bayoyong, Cabeldatan, Dumpay, Malimpec East, Mapolopolo, Nalneran, Navatat, Obong, Osmeña Sr., Palma, Patacbo and Poblacion. [PSA's Basista list](https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc/barangays/0105509000) and [Pangasinan's municipal profile](https://www.pangasinan.gov.ph/city-municipalities/basista/) were consulted for geographic context; the user's list controls application coverage.

## Implemented behavior

- **Clear**, **Streets** and **Dark** modes reduce clutter or show more road detail. An existing configured municipal style remains available. Choices persist on the device without storing incident data or credentials.
- Separate **Barangay**, **City/municipality** and **Province** switches display named Basista barangays, Basista with nearby municipality context, and Pangasinan province. City and barangay layers start enabled. Labels appear at suitable zoom levels; matching base-map place labels are suppressed while local barangay labels are shown, reducing duplicates.
- **Show hazard type** filters map markers to Fire, Road accident, Flooding or another catalog category. It shares the dashboard's category state and server query. Search/status filters still apply; expanding the map retains filters. Empty matches offer a category reset and disable Fit reports.
- **Basista area** returns to the service area. **Fit reports** fits the loaded report page. Counts and map-page controls identify paginated results; the map does not imply every report is loaded at once.
- Map style changes preserve the camera, incident pin, original GPS and report markers, and reinstall enabled overlays. A compact selector is also available on location maps. Errors offer a retry or another style; maps do not affect submission confirmation.
- New reports choose from the 13-barangay list, enforced by the API. Case/ASCII variants normalize, including `Osmena Sr.` → `Osmeña Sr.`. Other barangay names receive a validation error. A pin outside the reference extent prompts review; approximate borders are not used as a legal geofence.
- The additive camera migration changes only the previous generic Manila center and preserves customized settings. The existing local application retained all four accounts and its zero-report count. No incident record was deleted, renamed or reseeded.

## Boundary sources and phone delivery

The shipped geographic files total approximately **102 KB uncompressed**: 13 barangay features (370 original vertices, about 11 KB), six nearby municipal features (2,724 vertices, about 63 KB), and one province feature (about 28 KB). They are loaded as local assets when their layers are enabled; basemap tiles and fonts still require network access. Website builds use `/app/boundaries/`; Android builds include the files at the application asset root.

An initial coarse national extract was rejected during visual review because its straightened polygons were unsuitable for street-level use. Basista barangays and nearby municipalities were replaced with detailed WGS84 geographic layers from [altcoder's PSA/NAMRIA-derived shapefiles](https://github.com/altcoder/philippines-psgc-shapefiles), whose maintainer identifies the 2023 OCHA COD source. The original archives were checked against their published Git LFS SHA-256 values. Coordinates are rounded to six decimals for size; this is coordinate precision, not an accuracy claim. Pangasinan province context uses the [curated 2023 boundary release](https://github.com/bendlikeabamboo/barangay-boundaries-repository/releases/tag/v2026.4.13.0). Credit PSA, NAMRIA and the maintainers; do not treat the repositories' code licenses as a municipal survey approval.

[The asset provenance manifest](../hazard-client/public/boundaries/provenance.json) records selected features, counts, sizes and hashes. Large source archives and the pinned Python `pyshp==2.3.1` extraction tool stay in `.cache`, outside the phone/web bundle. Reproduce with `scripts/extract-basista-boundaries.py` after supplying its hash-verified archives and project-cache GIS reader; it finishes with the detailed extraction script. Never publish the large source archives as app assets.

Clear/Dark styles follow the [OpenFreeMap integration guide](https://openfreemap.org/quick_start/); Streets retains OpenStreetMap raster tiles and attribution. `VITE_MAP_STYLE_URL` preserves a municipal style; the three optional boundary URL variables can substitute approved GeoJSON FeatureCollections. `name` supplies boundary labels. Rebuild and synchronize after configuration changes.

Backend tests check scope rejection, canonical spelling and the conditional migration. Client/server lists and geographic names/hashes are checked together.

The real-provider browser review loaded Clear, Dark and Streets, local boundary assets and labels without page errors. Locked platform checks passed on PHP 8.5.1, both repositories passed whitespace checks, and changed source/artifact paths have Lenovo ownership with sampled access permissions preserved. Phone controls passed reflow and simulated 200% text at 320px; the location-map style test retained the confirmed incident pin. Initial PHP-fixture line-ending and test-selector failures were corrected and the full gate rerun successfully.

These are reference maps and local browser/source results.
